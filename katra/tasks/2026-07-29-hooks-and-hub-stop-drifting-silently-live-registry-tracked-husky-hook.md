Two defects that made katra's automation fail invisibly across 11 projects.

**Hub snapshotted the registry at startup.** `hubProjects()` was called once and
the slice closed over for the process lifetime. The hub runs as a login-time
launchd daemon with `KeepAlive`, so any katra registered afterwards never
appeared until a reboot — it was serving 9 of 11 registered projects. Now
re-read on an interval behind an `RWMutex`, with watchers started only for
newly-seen stores and the previous set retained on a load error.

**Husky hook installed into the generated directory.** Follow-on to
[[fix-hook-install-for-custom-core-hookspath-husky]], which correctly resolved
`core.hooksPath` to `.husky/_`. That directory is regenerated by `husky install`,
and worse, its shim ends in `exit $c` — so the block appended after the
`. "$(dirname "$0")/h"` line was unreachable. caststudio had never auto-stamped
once. Install now redirects to the tracked `.husky/post-commit` the shim execs.
`UninstallHook` was separately hardcoded to `.git/hooks`; both share `hookPath()`.
